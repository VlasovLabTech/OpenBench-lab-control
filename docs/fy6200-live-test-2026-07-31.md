## Hardware/API verification

### Basic dual-channel control

- All waveform codes `0..94` were written and read back on both channels:
  **190/190 passed**, no HTTP 503 responses after the serial-read optimization.
- Verified frequency limits:
  - sine: up to 20 MHz;
  - square: up to 15 MHz;
  - other built-in/ARB forms: up to 10 MHz.
- Verified frequency resolution down to 1 µHz.
- Verified offset from -10 V to +10 V, duty from 0.01% to 99.99%, and phase
  from 0° to 359.99°.
- Verified all four output combinations: OFF/OFF, ON/OFF, OFF/ON, ON/ON.
- Invalid channel/signal combinations are rejected before a write.

### Live-discovered amplitude limits

- The tested firmware clamps exactly 20.000 Vpp, but accepts 19.999 Vpp for
  sine, square, modulation, and ARB waveforms at or below 10 MHz.
- Built-in shaped waveforms 2–27 clamp values above 10 Vpp.
- OpenBench now validates these limits before writing, avoiding a partial
  hardware change followed by a misleading HTTP 503.

### Pulse width

- The command uses 10 ns units while read-back is expressed in nanoseconds.
- The tested hardware minimum is 100 ns, not 20 ns.
- Verified:
  - 100 ns at 5 MHz;
  - 1 s maximum at 0.5 Hz;
  - 100 µs restoration;
  - rejection when the effective width is equal to or longer than the period.

### Synchronization and advanced functions

- Synchronization: waveform, frequency, amplitude, offset, and duty were each
  enabled and disabled with read-back: **10/10 passed**.
- Burst: off, CH2, external, manual trigger, 1 cycle, 68 cycles, and 1,048,575
  cycles passed with read-back.
- FSK secondary frequency passed at 1 µHz, 20 kHz, and 10 MHz.
- ASK/FSK/PSK write commands passed, but this FY6200 firmware does not answer
  `RTA`, `RTF`, or `RTP`; source state therefore remains explicitly
  `unavailable`.
- Counter gate 1/10/100 s, AC/DC, and reset passed.
- With no counter input, frequency/count are zero and stale period/width/duty
  registers are now exposed as blank `idle` values.
- Sweep/VCO matrix: four targets × linear/logarithmic × time/VCO ×
  forward/reverse = **32/32 passed**.
- Sweep remains marked `verified: false` because this firmware exposes writes
  but no corresponding read-back.
- Ten negative API contract cases passed with the expected 400/422 response.
